Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

KATO: Afterimage is provided by Nathaniel Oh. This policy describes information handled by the game and by support.

The game

No game account is required. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, chat or user-generated content. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The developer does not collect personal data from gameplay or operate a game backend.

Local progress and preferences

The app stores campaign completion, best move counts, the last visited island and level, music volume and the current musical arrangement on your device. These values support gameplay and are not transmitted to a developer-operated server.

Purchases

Apple processes the one-time Full Version purchase through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify entitlement and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of purchase information.

Support requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond and investigate your request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Public issues and this website

GitHub issues are public, including your GitHub username and anything you post. Do not include personal information or receipts. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security according to its privacy statement. The site has no analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide crash diagnostics or aggregated App Store reports according to your Apple privacy settings. No third-party analytics or advertising SDK is enabled in the game.

Children

The app has no accounts, messaging or in-game personal-information submission form. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact the developer to request deletion.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the game or its information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For questions or requests, email kato.freefall@gmail.com.

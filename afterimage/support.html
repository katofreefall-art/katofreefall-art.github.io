A little help for your journey.

KATO: Afterimage is an isometric sliding-platform puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. Explore 1,000 levels across 200 islands, with an astronaut, a recon buggy and a limited oxygen supply.

Contact support

Email kato.freefall@gmail.com for help with the game, purchases or privacy.

You can also open a GitHub issue. Issues are public, so do not include personal information, passwords or purchase receipts.

How do I play?

Drag the platforms to clear a route from left to right. Each slide uses oxygen. In two-floor finales, move the buggy platform to the connector and use the arrows to boost between floors. Boosting does not use oxygen. The circular arrow restarts the puzzle; Home returns to the island map.

What is free?

The first 25 levels across five islands are free. After finishing them, tap the locked next island to see the Full Version offer. One purchase unlocks the full 1,000-level campaign. Islands still unlock through play.

Restore a purchase

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore purchases, using the Apple Account that made the original purchase. Apple handles the purchase and may ask you to authenticate. You do not need a separate game account.

Progress and sound

Progress and the musical arrangement are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app may remove local progress. Open Settings to adjust music volume. If you need help, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version and the island or level where the problem occurred.

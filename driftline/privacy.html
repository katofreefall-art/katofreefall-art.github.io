Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

KATO: Driftline is provided by Nathaniel Oh. This policy describes information handled by the game, this website and support.

The game

No game account is required. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics, chat or user-generated content. It does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. The developer does not collect personal data from gameplay or operate a game backend.

Local progress and preferences

The game stores mission progress, recovered astronaut counts, current location, discovery state, oxygen, control and audio preferences, musical state and permanent collection records on your device. These values support gameplay and are not transmitted to a developer-operated server. Reset Progress erases the local journey after confirmation. Deleting the app may remove local progress.

App Store download

The game is a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple handles download licensing and payment. The developer does not receive payment-card details or your Apple Account password. See Apple's privacy policy for Apple's handling of purchase information.

Support

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and information or attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond and investigate the request. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Public issues and this website

GitHub issues are public, including your GitHub username and anything you post. Do not include personal information or receipts. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security under its privacy statement. This site has no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. Opening the support or privacy link in the game opens this website in your browser.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide crash diagnostics or aggregated App Store reports according to your Apple privacy settings. No developer telemetry, third-party analytics or advertising SDK is enabled in the game.

Children

The game has no accounts, messaging or in-game personal-information submission form. Contact support if you believe a child sent personal information and you would like it deleted.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the game or its information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Questions and requests: kato.freefall@gmail.com.

Flight support

Help for KATO: Driftline on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email kato.freefall@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

You can also open a GitHub issue. Issues and attachments are public. Use email for private support.

Getting underway

Choose Play, then Deploy on the carrier console. Use the left stick to move, the right stick to change pitch and direction, and the centre slider for speed. Drag the open view to look around; release to recenter. Movement and look sensitivity can be adjusted in Settings.

Finding the lost

The cockpit radar tracks your current objective. Fly toward the contact and watch its height indicator. Tap a visible astronaut to recover them. Later missions begin at an abandoned ship. Approach and tap that ship to reveal possible recovery locations. A visible astronaut can be recovered even before you read the ship log.

Returning safely

After recovery, the radar follows your next destination. Fly into range and entry is automatic. Wait for the transfer to finish, then choose Deploy. Oxygen pauses near the carrier and refills while secured inside it.

Progress and resets

Progress saves locally when you return to the menu and at mission transitions. Reset Level returns to the current mission's carrier and keeps earlier recoveries. Settings > Reset Progress erases the entire journey after confirmation. Deleting the app can remove local progress. This version does not provide a developer cloud backup.

Sound and purchases

Music and sound effects have separate controls in Settings. Some parts of the journey deliberately use silence. The App Store download includes the complete game; there are no additional purchases to restore inside the app. Apple handles downloads, billing and refund requests through your Apple Account.

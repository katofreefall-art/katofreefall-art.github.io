KATO: Freefall

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

KATO: Freefall is provided by Nathaniel Oh. This policy describes information handled by the game and by support.

The game and versions covered

You do not need a game account. The game does not display advertisements and has no chat or user-generated content. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or device location.

Version 1.0.1 and earlier do not include third-party gameplay analytics. Version 1.0.2 adds optional advertising measurement. These features apply only to versions that contain them.

Optional advertising measurement

On iPhone and iPad, version 1.0.2 requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, the game initializes Meta's SDK and shares app activation information, campaign and Endless run activity, world progress, campaign completion, and new verified Full Version purchases with Meta Platforms. Purchase events include the product, the verified transaction amount and currency, and the sandbox or production store environment. Restoring a purchase does not report a new sale.

Meta's SDK may process device and app-instance identifiers, including the Apple advertising identifier when authorized, technical information about your app and device, network information such as your IP address and approximate location derived from it, and SDK diagnostic information. We do not provide Meta with your name, email, contacts, Apple Account credentials, payment-card details, or a Facebook login. The information is used to measure Freefall advertisements, understand progression and purchases, and help reach people who may enjoy the game. Meta processes shared information under Meta's Privacy Policy and Meta's Business Tools Terms.

Your choice

If you decline tracking, have not made a choice, or your device restricts tracking, the game does not initialize Meta's SDK or send these app events to Meta. All game features and purchased content remain available regardless of your choice. Settings > Ad Privacy opens the game's iOS settings, where you can review your choice. You can also review tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Changes apply to future measurement and do not automatically delete information already received by Meta.

The game also uses Apple's SKAdNetwork install-attribution API. Apple may provide delayed, privacy-preserving aggregate attribution information under its own rules, independently of your tracking permission. It does not give the developer access to your identity.

Data storage and privacy requests

KATO does not operate a server storing individual gameplay telemetry. Meta processes received information under its own retention and privacy policies. You can use Meta's privacy controls or contact the developer below with a privacy request. We will explain what we can identify or delete and any limits of our access to information held by Apple or Meta. Transaction identifiers used to prevent duplicate reporting remain on your device and are not included in our purchase event parameters.

Local preferences

The game stores settings and progress information on your device, such as music volume, control preferences, world selection, run seeds, and campaign completion. These values support gameplay and are not sent to a developer-operated game server.

Purchases

Apple processes Full Version purchases through the App Store. The game uses StoreKit to verify purchase entitlement and restore access. The developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Public bug reports

If you open a GitHub issue, your GitHub username and anything you post there are publicly visible. Do not put personal information, purchase receipts, or payment details in an issue. Use the support email for private questions.

Support requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information or attachments you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Do not include passwords or payment-card details. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below.

Diagnostics and this website

Apple may provide crash diagnostics or aggregated App Store reports according to your Apple privacy settings. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub handles that information according to its privacy statement. This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics scripts.

Children

The game does not provide accounts, messaging, or an in-game personal-information submission form. Optional advertising measurement is not intended for children under 13, and Apple's restricted tracking status is respected. If you believe information about a child has been shared through support or measurement, contact the developer so we can investigate and address the request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the game or its information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email kato.freefall@gmail.com.
